The Problem

Drilling operations run on data no one is allowed to send to the cloud

Stuck-pipe events, non-productive time, and third-party data exposure are three faces of the same underlying problem: operators need intelligence built on their own telemetry, without handing that telemetry to someone else's model.

⚠

Stuck-pipe risk

A stuck-pipe event can halt operations for days and put both the asset and the crew at risk. Early, physically-grounded warning — not a black-box score — is the highest-leverage intervention available to a driller.

⏳

Non-productive time

NPT from stuck pipe and related drag events is routinely one of the largest controllable cost drivers on a well. Catching the precursor signature meters before the event, not after it, is the difference that matters.

🔒

Data sovereignty

Sending live drilling telemetry to a third-party cloud API isn't acceptable for many operators. TENETDrill fine-tunes and serves its model entirely on-premise — the data never leaves the rig network.

Architecture

One pipeline, entirely on-premise

Enterprise telemetry is refined into an Institutional Language Model, specialized further into an organization-specific model, and orchestrated by a Supervisor Agent that composes the final driller-facing decision — grounded in physics rules and validated detector output, not a black box.
